# Roadmap

CrewScore is an offline, deterministic checker for safeguards written into AI-agent system prompts and for configuration smells in coding-agent files. Its public roadmap separates implemented behavior from proposed research and maintenance so plans are not mistaken for shipped safety claims.

## Current public core

- An open catalog of 23 written controls across eight governance-signal dimensions.
- Offline CLI, GitHub Action, and browser checker with no LLM or API key in the scoring path.
- Prompt-free machine output by default and explicit refusal to treat coding-agent configuration as a governance score.
- A reproducible corpus of 356 public prompts, including 83 production-labeled prompts, used to study coverage separation rather than runtime quality.
- Disclosed weak construct validity for Cost, Compliance, and Audit, plus a public record of the withdrawn earlier corpus study.

## Bounded research and maintenance work

These items are proposed work. They are not funded, shipped, or promised on a fixed schedule.

1. **Expand and document the public corpus.** Add consented or clearly licensed prompt examples, preserve source/provenance fields, version the sampling protocol, and publish exclusions and missing data.
2. **Independent control review.** Ask external reviewers to examine control wording, false positives, false negatives, and construct validity. Publish disagreements and negative findings rather than averaging them away.
3. **Map controls to current public frameworks.** Produce a versioned crosswalk to relevant OWASP agentic and language-model guidance. A mapping is documentation, not compliance or certification.
4. **Per-control validation evidence.** Replace broad dimension claims with measured evidence for individual controls where possible, and weaken or remove controls whose validity cannot be supported.
5. **Optional ecosystem adapters.** Add prompt-free export or CI adapters only when they preserve the offline engine, open rules, and explicit distinction between written coverage and runtime behavior.

## Validation gates

Every scoring change must update the public rule catalog, web export, tests, changelog, and validation material. Corpus results must be generated by the committed harness. New claims about safety, adoption, runtime behavior, legal compliance, or outcomes require direct evidence and are out of scope for documentation-only work.

## Non-goals

- Safety certification, compliance attestation, or a ranking of prompts, teams, vendors, or agents.
- Claims that written controls are obeyed at runtime.
- Hosted prompt collection, background telemetry, or an LLM dependency in the core checker.
- Rebranding known-thin Cost, Compliance, or Audit matches as validated outcomes.
- Replacing runtime adversarial tools such as Promptfoo or garak.
